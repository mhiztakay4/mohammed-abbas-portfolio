WEB DEVELOPMENT

Why a mobile-first website matters

Many visitors first encounter a website on a phone. A mobile-first approach starts by making the most important content, navigation and actions work well on a small screen, then expands the layout for larger displays.

That means readable text, comfortable tap targets, images that fit the screen and forms that are easy to complete. It also means testing on real devices instead of assuming a desktop layout will automatically translate.

GRAPHIC DESIGN

Good design starts with clarity

A design can be colourful and attractive yet still fail if people cannot understand its message. Clear hierarchy helps viewers notice the most important information first.

Consistent spacing, readable type, purposeful colour and a strong focal point make a design easier to understand. Decoration should support the message, not compete with it.

PROJECT PROCESS

From idea to a useful digital product

Start by describing the problem in plain language and identifying who experiences it. Then decide what the first useful version needs to do, rather than trying to build every possible feature at once.

Build a simple version, test the important user journeys, listen to feedback and improve the parts that cause confusion. A useful product grows through clear priorities and repeated refinement.
